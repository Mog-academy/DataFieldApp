03

Pain Points

Custody, connectivity, and TPM shape. These are the problems a generic tool will not solve in rural Sudan.

What problems does this solve?

Data is not really yours. Kobo/Google hold the project, media, and access trail. Offboarding, audit, and “delete our data from their cloud” become political.
Rural Sudan breaks online forms. Google Forms needs a live connection. Teams lose interviews, batch-submit in towns, or fall back to paper and re-entry error.
Generic tools ignore TPM shape. 40 facilities, 15 states, 4 tools, 4 teams, 10 field days, GPS + photos, 260 exit interviews. Kobo can store answers; it does not run this operation.
Supervisors fly blind. Completeness vs target is a spreadsheet after the fact, not a live board during Days 1–10.
Brand leakage. Every tablet opens Kobo. Partners remember the tool, not MindAct.
Arabic + protection interviews are awkward. Tool 4 needs consent, PSEA-safe close, no probing. Generic builders do not encode that duty of care.
Analysis is bolted on. Charts in Kobo/Forms are shallow. Facility × IP × tool triangulation for WHO reports is manual and slow.
Electricity and media. Uncompressed photos and chatty cloud apps drain batteries and fail on weak 3G. The product must queue, compress, and retry.

Control is the product.

05

How It Works

Collect in the field. Queue when the signal dies. Analyze on mind-act.co when you are back online.

How will the app work?

Enumerator offline→ Queue + sync→ MindAct admin→ Client dashboard→ WHO exports

Supervisor feeds the same admin layer. Four surfaces. One data store.

1. Field app

Android phones/tablets. Login or QR setup. Assigned facilities. Tools 1–4. Drafts offline. Required GPS and compressed photos. Finalize into a sync queue. Auto-retry when online.

2. Supervisor

Teams A–D board. Completeness matrix (facility × tool). Pending sync. Revisit / access issues. State debrief notes.

3. Ops admin

On mind-act.co: projects, 40-facility roster, form versions, users/roles, raw submissions, media, access log. This is the ownership layer.

4. Client dashboard

Round command center (10 hospitals / 16 PHC / 10 SC / 260 exits), GPS map, facility dossier, QA flags, clean dataset + round report. Badge: Hosted by MindAct.

Field reality rules

  • No beneficiary self-fill on a public website. Enumerator-led.
  • Paper fallback IDs if a device dies; enter later under the same case ID.
  • Sync hubs: locality capital, overnight, or Days 7–10 QA window.
  • Arabic RTL in the field; English (bilingual-ready) on the client dashboard.

Offline first. 

MindAct always.

07

User Experience

Enumerator, exit interview, supervisor, client. Each path is designed for the 10-day TPM reality.

How will people use it?

1. Hospital assessment

Login → My work → Facility → Tool 1 → Sections A–N with draft save → GPS → Photos → Finalize → Queue → Sync later.

2. Exit interview

Consent → Demographics → Access → Protection → Close with feedback channel → Finalize with no name stored.

3. Supervisor day check

Team board → Filter state/team → Incomplete facilities / pending sync → Flag revisit → Debrief note.

4. Client ownership

Login → Targets vs actual → GPS map → Facility dossier → Export → Ownership badge and access log.

Non-negotiables

  • Cannot finalize without required GPS when marked.
  • Sync failure is retryable, never silent data loss.
  • Tool 4: no probing of alleged perpetrators; PSEA path if payment/gift is disclosed.
  • Client landing is one headline + one CTA, not a card dump.

Four journeys. One round.

How those ranges are built

Small regional studio. A 2–4 person shop commonly bills about AED 80–180/hour blended. This scope is roughly 200–400 studio hours. That is how you reach AED 35k–80k.

Freelance full-stack. A capable regional/remote contractor often charges about AED 55–150/hour ($15–40/hr), or AED 4,000–12,000/month. A tight build is 180–350 focused hours. That is how you reach AED 15k–45k, usually without a dedicated designer or QA.

In-house hire. A full-stack employee in Sudan/Egypt is often AED 4,000–12,000/month. Two to four months of build is AED 8,000–48,000 in salary before you have a finished v1, and you still carry the person after launch.
