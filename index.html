01 · Purpose

Own the field data pipeline.

MindAct DataField is a purpose-built survey and TPM platform for MindAct’s field work in Sudan and beyond. Enumerators collect hospital, PHC, stabilization centre, and beneficiary exit interviews offline, then sync to MindAct’s own infrastructure on mind-act.co.

Offline-first Arabic field UX MindAct hosting

02 · Problem

Not a missing form.
Missing control.

Custody, connectivity, and TPM shape. Generic tools will not solve this in rural Sudan.

Data is not yours

Kobo/Google hold the project, media, and access trail.

Rural Sudan breaks online forms

Teams lose interviews, batch-submit in towns, or fall back to paper.

Generic tools ignore TPM

40 facilities, 15 states, 4 tools, 4 teams, 10 field days need an OS, not a form builder.

03 · Solution

A MindAct-owned field OS.

DataField puts WHO-ECHO TPM data on MindAct infrastructure, with offline Arabic collection and a branded client dashboard.

  • Facility roster, Teams A–D, Tools 1–4
  • Offline store, GPS, photos, retryable sync
  • Supervisor completeness + admin + client view
  • Source and hosting under MindAct control
