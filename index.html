01 · Purpose

Data Ownership

MindAct DataField is a purpose-built survey and TPM platform for MindAct’s field work in Sudan and beyond. Enumerators collect hospital, PHC, stabilization centre, and beneficiary exit interviews offline, then sync to MindAct’s own infrastructure on mind-act.co.

Offline-first Arabic field UX MindAct hosting

02 · Problem

Why build an app?

Using external tools is fine, but:

Data is not yours

Kobo/Google hold the project, media, and access trail.

Rural Sudan breaks online forms

Teams lose interviews, batch-submit in towns, or fall back to paper.

Generic tools ignore TPM

40 facilities, 15 states, 4 tools, 4 teams, 10 field days need an OS, not a form builder.

03 · Solution

Benefits to MindAct:

  • Data Ownership: WHO-ECHO and future project data sit on MindAct-controlled hosting. No Kobo account dependency, no Google Forms custody.
  • Branding = client confidence: Clients can be given access to the app's dashboard and one-click export.
  • Win more work: Having your own app can be a real differentiator vs consultancies who only subcontract Kobo.
  • Speed: Facility roster, team assignment (A–D), tool picker (1–4), and 10-day completeness are built for MindAct’s actual TPM workflow.
